A Sanctuary in Varanasi

Where Cantonment Calm Meets Cosmopolitan Taste

Located in the serene Buddha Vihar Colony near Hotel Rio Benaras, Cafe 55 was born from a passion for elevated café culture. Away from the congested bustling ghats, we provide a sophisticated green retreat where freshly ground Arabica meets world-class artisanal delicacies.

From our acclaimed breakfast platters and cheesy baked continental delights to our signature hand-poured coffees and handcrafted Italian thin crusts, every plate is prepared with uncompromising ingredient integrity and warm Benarasi hospitality.
